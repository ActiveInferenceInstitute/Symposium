## Submitted abstract

Discovering whether there is a minimum structure required for inquiry to be complete&#46;
The paper begins with six familiar questions&#58; Who&#44; What&#44; Where&#44; When&#44; How&#44; and Why&#46;
But the research is asking something deeper&#58; Are these six categories simply a useful convention&#44; or does each represent something fundamentally necessary about what can be known&#63;
Consider the bridge&#45;collapse example&#46;
Two bridge collapses occur&#46; For both&#44; the involved parties are identified
&#40;WHO&#58; the same engineering firm and inspection authority in both cases&#41;&#46; The event is
specified &#40;WHAT&#58; structural failure of a suspension bridge&#41;&#46; The locations are of the
same type &#40;WHERE&#58; bridges of identical design crossing rivers of similar flow rate&#41;&#46; The
time and sequence of failure are identical &#40;WHEN&#58; both collapse within a three&#45;minute
window following the same triggering load&#41;&#46; The mechanical failure sequence is
identical in both cases &#40;HOW&#58; the same crack propagation pattern&#44; the same load
transfer failure&#44; the same sequence of component failures&#41;&#46;
Even with all five answers fixed and identical across both collapses&#44; the explanation
differs&#58; in the first case&#44; deferred maintenance created metal fatigue that made the
bridge susceptible to normal load&#59; in the second&#44; a design flaw in the original
specifications made the bridge inherently susceptible under identical conditions&#46; The
identical HOW does not determine WHY&#46; The joint answer set does not determine
WHY&#46; Therefore WHY is independent of WHO&#44; WHAT&#44; WHERE&#44; WHEN&#44; and HOW&#46;
