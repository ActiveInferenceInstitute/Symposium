# GNN in 2026&#58; Generalized Notation Notation as a Specification Layer for Applied Active Inference

## Submitted abstract

Generalized Notation Notation &#40;GNN&#41; was introduced in 2023 &#40;Smekal &#38; Friedman&#41; as a text&#45;based standard for writing Active Inference generative models&#44; with a &#8220;Triple Play&#8221; of linguistic&#44; visual and executable renderings&#46; This talk reports where GNN stands in 2026 and what it enables for applied Active Inference&#46;
A GNN file is plain Markdown&#58; a state&#45;space block declaring variables and dimensions&#44; a connections graph&#44; initial parameterization &#40;e&#46;g&#46; A&#44; B&#44; C&#44; D&#44; E and related terms&#41;&#44; equations&#44; time settings and Active Inference Ontology annotations&#46; The open&#45;source GNN pipeline &#40;v5&#44; 25 steps&#41; parses&#44; type&#45;checks and validates these files&#44; visualizes and exports them&#44; maps them to the ontology&#44; renders them to backends including PyMDP&#44; RxInfer&#46;jl&#44; ActiveInference&#46;jl and JAX&#44; executes the results&#44; and reports each run against source hashes and configuration&#46; The repository&#8217;s 38 exemplar models span static and dynamic perception&#44; discrete POMDPs&#44; the epistemic T&#45;maze&#44; continuous and hybrid models&#44; hierarchical and deep temporal models&#44; Dirichlet learning&#44; precision and curiosity&#44; and multi&#45;agent stigmergic swarms&#46;
The core is GNN as the specification layer for real projects&#46; The Active Inference Language Model &#40;AILM&#44; released October 2026&#41; uses GNN to specify each layer of a six&#45;level hierarchical discrete language system &#40;letters&#44; phonemes&#44; words&#44; case roles&#44; sentence types&#44; document position&#41;&#44; executed in pymdp with no transformer&#44; neural network or learned embedding&#44; and checked with a gate&#45;audit method that tests claims against trivial baselines and deliberately broken variants and ties each reported number to its recorded run&#46; Shorter case studies&#58; deterministic codebase&#45;to&#45;GNN translation &#40;COGANT&#41;&#44; inference over assembly traces &#40;Active Assembly&#41;&#44; Battleship&#44; and gratitude and model revisability &#40;Thanks for Nothing&#41;&#46;
Throughout&#44; I distinguish what a GNN declaration establishes &#40;an inspectable&#44; reproducible model&#41; from what needs separate evidence &#40;backend support&#44; numerical acceptance&#44; empirical claims&#41;&#46; I close with open problems and invitations for contributors&#58; new exemplars&#44; backends&#44; ontology mappings and teaching materials&#46;
