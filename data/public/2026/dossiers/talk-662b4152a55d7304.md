# Percepia&#58; Perceptual Phenotyping from Conversation

Presenter: Patricia Craja

Contribution: Pre&#45;recorded Video presentation

Public ID: talk&#45;662b4152a55d7304

## Submitted abstract

Psychological taxonomies&#44; from the Big Five to the DSM&#44; are descriptive&#44; not mechanistic&#58; they classify how people differ without modelling why&#46; Active inference offers a mechanistic account&#58; it formalises a small set of generative parameters that govern how a mind perceives&#44; learns and acts under uncertainty&#46; Chief among them is precision&#58; the confidence a mind places in different sources of information&#46; Sensory and prior precision govern whether new evidence or existing expectations dominate belief updating&#59; policy precision&#44; how confidently a mind selects goal&#45;directed action over habit&#59; preference precision&#44; how it balances reward&#45;seeking against information&#45;seeking&#46; Their configuration constitutes what we call a mind&#8217;s perceptual phenotype&#46; Meta&#45;precision&#44; a mind&#8217;s capacity to monitor and revise its own precision assignments&#44; has been proposed as the mechanism underlying epistemic depth &#8212; one of three conditions the theory identifies for consciousness &#40;Laukkonen et al&#46; 2025&#41;&#46; Today first&#45;order precision parameters are estimated from laboratory tasks&#59; meta&#45;precision has no established paradigm&#46;
This talk introduces Percepia&#44; a perceptual phenotyping layer between a user and a large language model that tracks probability distributions over personality and beliefs&#44; and aims to reverse&#45;infer the precision parameters from them&#46;
I present two experiments with synthetic LLM personas&#44; where parameters are known by construction&#46; In the first&#44; 625 personas were prompted to vary sensory&#44; prior and policy precision in conversation&#59; Percepia inferred their Big Five from the dialogue&#44; testing whether precision leaves a recoverable signature in personality&#44; as Safron and DeYoung &#40;2021&#41; propose&#46; In the second&#44; personas varied in meta&#45;precision&#44; and Percepia tracked their stage of adult development &#40;Kegan 1994&#41;&#46;
As the personas were primed with the theory&#44; these experiments test whether a reverse&#45;inference channel from language to precision parameters is viable&#44; not whether the mapping holds in humans&#58; a first step toward measuring perceptual phenotypes at scale&#46;
