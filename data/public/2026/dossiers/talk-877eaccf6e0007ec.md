# ROIF and Active Inference&#58; The Formal Boundary of History&#45;Conditioned Anticipatory Systems&#46;

History&#45;dependent reconfiguration&#44; structured memory&#44; and predictive preconfiguration in prestressed complex systems

Presenter: Vitalii Shapovalov

## Submitted abstract

Recursive Organic Integration Framework &#40;ROIF&#41; is a deterministic&#44; domain&#45;agnostic computational architecture for complex systems whose present organization and subsequent responses may be shaped by prior interactions&#44; distributed reconfiguration&#44; and anticipatory preconfiguration&#46; Rather than treating history as an additional input variable&#44; ROIF represents how prior events may become embodied in the organization through which subsequent events are processed&#46;
The framework distinguishes experimentally separable routes through which the past and represented possible futures may affect subsequent dynamics&#58; history embodied in present organization&#59; structured memory conditioning a subsequent transition&#59; and anticipatory preconfiguration based on a represented possible future Temporal Image&#46; Matched&#45;current&#45;state controls found no independently identifiable history&#45;specific transition effect for the tested physical&#45;history pathway&#46; In contrast&#44; a separate structured&#45;memory experiment produced different subsequent physical transitions under matched represented physical state and adaptive connection state&#46;
The predictive architecture allows possible future SystemImages to participate in present preconfiguration&#46; Current experiments&#44; however&#44; test only a restricted one&#45;step preconfiguration within the implemented prestress representation&#46; This experimental restriction does not define the broader architectural role of prestress in ROIF&#44; where prestress reflects an already organized relational system state shaped by interacting internal and external conditions&#44; constraints&#44; capacities&#44; history&#44; and time&#46; Objective&#45;independence tests further prevent interpreting the present result as evidence for general predictive stabilization&#46;
These results motivate a boundary question rather than a claim of equivalence&#58; when does a history&#45;conditioned anticipatory architecture constitute Active Inference&#63; ROIF does not presently require a variational free&#45;energy functional&#44; Bayesian generative model&#44; or particular neurobiological implementation&#46; The presentation therefore uses ROIF as a computational case study to ask which mathematical conditions distinguish Active Inference from a broader class of history&#45;conditioned anticipatory systems&#44; and whether these formalisms should be understood as nested&#44; neighbouring&#44; or formally distinct&#46;
