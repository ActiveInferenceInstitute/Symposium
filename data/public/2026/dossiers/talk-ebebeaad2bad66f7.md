# From Minimal Regulators to World Models&#58; Boundary&#45;Relative Distinctions for Factoring Generative Models

Presenter: Jesse Parent &#38; Avery Lim

Contribution: Pre&#45;recorded Video presentation

Public ID: talk&#45;ebebeaad2bad66f7

## Submitted abstract

A good regulator&#8217;s model is a reduced representation that preserves useful variety &#40;Alicea&#44; Hough&#44; Nelson and Parent&#44; 2026&#41;&#46; For active inference&#44; this raises a design question&#58; before any content is learned&#44; which distinctions between sources and channels should an agent&#8217;s generative model keep apart&#63;
We propose two taxonomies drawn from minimal regulators&#46; Causal Standpoint Routes&#44; derived from Turing&#8217;s networked machine&#44; classify any influence across a boundary by where it starts&#44; where it ends&#44; and whether it passes through another system&#46; The four immediate routes are the Markov blanket&#8217;s own dependencies&#46; Four mediated routes cover reports relayed through another agent&#44; actions delegated to tools&#44; chains of external causes&#44; and stored state&#46; Allostatic Kinds&#44; derived from Ashby&#8217;s homeostat&#44; describe what an agent regulates through each coupling&#58; set points as preferred outcomes&#44; the sign of each coupling&#44; and allostasis when preferences shift&#46; Both are relative to where the boundary is drawn&#44; which is what lets them carry across domains&#46;
The routes add no new inference machinery&#46; They propose how to factor the model so that existing machinery&#44; such as epistemic value and novelty&#44; targets the right channels&#58; probing an intermediary&#44; for example&#44; rather than only the world behind it&#46; Merging channels predicts specific failures&#46; Internally generated content treated as sensory fits the precision account of hallucination&#46; In language models&#44; prompt injection&#44; invented sources and long&#45;context drift look like the same breakdowns&#46;
We outline two tests&#58; pymdp agents factored by route&#44; compared under environmental shift with agents factored by matched irrelevant distinctions&#59; and whether reinforcing these distinctions in LLM agents reduces source confusion more than unrelated errors&#46; We present proposals and study designs&#44; not results&#44; and put one open claim to the audience&#58; that mediated routes correspond to nested blankets and deeper levels of the generative model&#46;
