# Coupling energy&#45;system models with a seam you can read and an agent you can audit&#58; Active Inference in the NEST programme

## Submitted abstract

Decarbonisation decisions span scales no single model covers&#46; Energy&#45;system&#44; city and building&#45;stock models must exchange results despite different assumptions and resolutions&#46; Manual coupling depends on expert judgement&#59; LLM orchestration adds a need for inspectable decisions&#46; NEST&#44; a three&#45;year Schmidt Sciences DEVI programme led by Open Earth Foundation with IIASA&#44; CEU and the Active Inference Institute&#44; makes those boundaries explicit&#46;
First&#44; the seam&#46; Each exchanged variable carries a declared unit&#44; convention&#44; geography and mapping rule sourced to its owner&#46; IIASA&#8217;s MESSAGEix&#8211;STURM boundary for Western Europe reveals why this matters&#58; two solvers reach the same objective but different fuel prices&#44; making solver choice part of the interface&#46;
Second&#44; uncertainty&#46; We are testing generative models specified in GNN&#44; with RxInfer as the first backend&#44; to carry beliefs across seams&#46; Cross&#45;backend equivalence and free&#45;energy&#45;based coupling criteria remain to be tested&#46; Known&#45;precision synthetic controls pass latent&#45;state calibration&#59; introducing parameter uncertainty produces failures in both states and noise precisions&#46; Those uncertainty estimates are not yet qualified for real coupling outputs&#46;
Third&#44; the agent&#44; audited&#46; OEF&#8217;s durable orchestrator exports a trace contract that AII&#8217;s independent consumer checks for schema compatibility&#44; hash integrity and accounting reconciliation&#46; Two real exports&#44; scripted and LLM&#45;driven&#44; pass those checks&#46; These checks do not validate the energy&#45;model computation or establish autonomous discovery of a coupling method&#46;
We close with a proposed federation of project agents across the DEVI cohort&#58; models declare their boundaries&#44; couplings leave inspectable records&#44; and groups can reason about systems no participant sees whole&#46;
