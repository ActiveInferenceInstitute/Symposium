## Submitted abstract

Winnicott held that the infant&#8217;s transitional object must never be questioned &#45; did you create it or find it&#63; The paradox is to be held&#44; not resolved&#44; because play&#44; creativity and culture live in the space it opens&#46; This talk argues that active inference carries the same paradox&#46; Perception is jointly authored by the generative model and the evidence&#44; and action shapes the world the agent then finds&#46; I draw thinking from the pioneers of early childhood education&#44; philosophy of time and contemporary discussions&#46; I explore some possible consequences&#44; treating the unresolved paradox as a design principle&#46;
