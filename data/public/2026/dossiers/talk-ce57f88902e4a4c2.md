# Root Frequency Theory&#58; An Integrative Framework for the Continuity of Lived Experience

## Submitted abstract

How do we keep a coherent sense of self across time &#8212; and can that process be measured in real time&#44; on consumer hardware&#44; before it breaks down&#63;
Root Frequency Theory &#40;RFT&#41; proposes that experiential continuity is not a default state but an ongoing biological achievement requiring coordination across five nested regulatory layers&#58; environmental constraints &#40;C0&#41;&#44; autopoietic regulation &#40;C1&#41;&#44; predictive neural integration &#40;C2&#41;&#44; narrative self&#45;organization &#40;C3&#41;&#44; and the continuity of lived experience &#40;C4&#41;&#46; Fragmentation is defined as a self&#45;reinforcing cascade of cross&#45;scale misalignment&#46; The cascade is bidirectional&#58; structured self&#45;knowledge introduced at C3 &#8212; operationalized as epistemic alignment &#40;Kₑ&#41; &#8212; is hypothesized to initiate upward regulatory stabilization&#46;
To test this&#44; I developed the M&#45;RFT metric&#58; M &#61; &#40;Φ &#183; Kₑ&#41; &#47; F&#44; where Φ indexes autonomic flexibility via HRV&#44; Kₑ composites interoceptive accuracy and narrative coherence&#44; and F captures regulatory burden&#46; I then built Root Extension &#8212; an iOS application using iPhone and Apple Watch &#8212; to administer this protocol ecologically and deliver personalized epistemic scaffolding upon session completion&#46;
The N&#61;1 longitudinal study ran 321 sessions across 23 active days &#40;April 16&#8211;May 14&#44; 2026&#41;&#44; with the researcher as the sole subject&#46; Key findings&#58; mean M &#61; 3&#46;79 &#40;Mdn &#61; 2&#46;14&#41;&#44; intra&#45;session regulatory recovery &#8212; FRAGMENTED to RESILIENT within the same calendar day &#8212; observed on 34&#46;8&#37; of active days&#46;
This presentation introduces the theoretical architecture&#44; the metric&#44; the app protocol&#44; and the preliminary data &#8212; and invites the active inference community into the next phase&#58; a formal N&#61;20&#8211;40 validation trial planned for 2027&#46;
