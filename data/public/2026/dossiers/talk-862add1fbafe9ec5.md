# Commongrounds AI&#58; alife&#45;inspired virtual environments for embodied active inference agents

Presenter: Nick Hay&#44; Adam Safron

Contribution: Pre&#45;recorded Video presentation

Public ID: talk&#45;862add1fbafe9ec5

## Submitted abstract

Common Grounds AI is making virtual worlds where humans and AIs can inhabit simulated bodies and playfully pursue open&#45;ended goals together&#46; Each world is a rich but simplified analog of our own&#44; using material physics simulations to ground core aspects of human experience&#46;
Today&#8217;s AIs are largely trained on fragmented&#44; ungrounded text that conveys a distorted&#44; partial version of human conceptual ontology&#46; We are closing this ontological gap by building rich virtual playgrounds where AIs and humans can share social and physical experiences&#44; grounding language in embodied interactions that mirror young children&#8217;s cognitive development&#46; Our most recent prototype models temperature and chemical reactions&#46; It also includes experimental examples of native creatures that consume environmental free energy and move using muscle analogues&#44; with plans for action&#45;perception loops guided by Active Inference principles&#46; We plan to explore whether incorporating embodiment yields systems that have ontologies more compatible with human cognition&#44; both by fine&#45;tuning LLMs on embodied experience and by building hybrid architectures merging the environment&#8217;s native creatures with LLMs&#46;
Our virtual worlds will be broadly accessible &#40;playable in a browser&#44; no installation needed&#41;&#44; released as open&#45;source software&#44; and &#40;we hope&#41; fun to play&#46; We want these worlds to serve as a common ground for humans and many forms of AI and AI research to meet&#46;
