## Submitted abstract

The Rosales&#45;Gheuens framework challenges the assumption that brute&#45;force energy alone saves open systems from collapse&#46; In this session&#44; we will introduce a testable&#44; normalized physics layer that merges the Rosales congruence principle with Gheuens&#8217; interaction geometry&#46; Rather than replacing established physical laws&#44; this framework acts as a powerful diagnostic tool that sits cleanly on top of them&#46; Our core master equation posits that a system&#39;s survival requires geometrically aligned effective recovery multiplied by a structural congruence gate&#44; minus total perturbation&#46;  
Crucially&#44; we will explore how this model bridges directly to Karl Friston&#8217;s Free Energy Principle&#46; Friston dictates that systems persist by minimizing variational free energy&#44; or &#34;surprise&#46;&#34; We will demonstrate how our framework translates this active inference into hard physical variables&#44; mathematically formalizing how a system must continuously minimize structural mismatch and physical dissipation to avoid shattering&#46;
To ground the theory empirically&#44; we will use Quantum Error Correction &#40;QEC&#41; as our primary physical anchor&#44; mapping code decoders and physical error rates directly to aligned recovery and perturbation&#46;  
Interactive Aspect&#58;The session will conclude with a collaborative exercise where attendees can apply the framework&#39;s &#34;falsification matrix&#34; to a theoretical open system&#44; testing how our bounded composite equation predicts system survival compared to simpler Markovian baselines&#46;
