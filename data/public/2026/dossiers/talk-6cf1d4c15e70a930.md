## Submitted abstract

This project introduces an innovative computational architecture that bridges cognitive neuroscience and interactive simulation by integrating na Active Inference cognitive core directly into UI engines&#46; Grounded in the Free Energy Principle&#44; the system continuously minimizes Variational Free Energy &#40;VFE&#41; to drive autonomous agent behavior&#44; dynamic navigation&#44; and adaptive decision&#45;making within complex&#44; changing environments&#46;
Unlike traditional rule&#45;based non&#45;player characters &#40;NPCs&#41; or standard robotic controllers&#44; the agent maintains an internal affective engine that maps multi&#45;dimensional emotional telemetry&#8212;such as curiosity&#44; motivation&#44; stress&#44; and fear&#8212;directly to behavioral drives&#44; energy management&#44; and rich audio&#45;visual expressions&#46; These affective states are rendered in real time through belief&#45;versus&#45;reality spatial overlays&#44; floating emotional telemetry indicators&#44; and adaptive state vocalizations that mirrors the agent&#39;s psychological trajectory&#46;
By unifying autonomous perception&#45;action loops with transparent emotional expression&#44; this framework offers powerful capabilities for powering both advanced games and autonomous robots&#46; Its potential applications span multiple domains&#58;
Entertainment&#58; Powering next&#45;generation autonomous game characters and dynamic simulations with emergent&#44; lifelike behaviors&#46;
Education&#58; Serving as an interactive pedagogical platform for exploring cognitive architectures&#44; cybernetics&#44; and affective computing&#46;
Companionship &#38; Emotional Support&#58; Creating empathetic digital companions and social robots capable of contextual awareness and authentic emotional resonance to foster meaningful human&#45;agent interaction and support human well&#45;being&#46;
Ultimately&#44; this project demonstrates that Active Inference provides a robust&#44; biologically inspired foundation for artificial systems that not only navigate physical spaces intelligently but also communicate and connect through shared affective experiences&#46;
