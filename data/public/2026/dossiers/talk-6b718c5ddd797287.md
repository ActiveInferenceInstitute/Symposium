Presenter: Ryan Smith 

Contribution: Livestreamed panel or group session

## Submitted abstract

In this talk&#44; I will review recent studies and results using active inference and related modeling approaches to study mechanisms of normative cognitive&#45;affective processes and associated neural responses&#44; and how these appear to be affected in different psychiatric conditions&#46; I will also discuss contexts in which active inference appears to offer potentially unique resources for modeling these processes&#46; Conversely&#44; I will discuss contexts in which active inference may overlap with simpler modeling approaches and related issues centered around the level of mechanistic description one is interested in studying&#46;
