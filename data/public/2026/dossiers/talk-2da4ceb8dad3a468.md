## Submitted abstract

Consciousness&#58; The Channel Framework and its Application to INTREPID
The Channel Framework treats theories of consciousness as relating physical and experiential events&#44; with the minimal relation being predictive&#46; The Channel Framework embeds this psychophysical relation within the full experimental prediction architecture&#44; connecting physical processes&#44; conscious content&#44; introspective access&#44; and observable outcomes&#46; Expressed through probabilistic channels&#44; these architectures can be interpreted as generative models of experimental predictions&#44; providing a common language in which different theories can be explicated and compared&#46;
I apply the framework to INTREPID&#8217;s adversarial comparison of Integrated Information Theory and predictive&#45;processing theories of consciousness&#44; including Neurorepresentationalism and the Active Inference theory of consciousness &#40;AI&#45;C&#41;&#46; The framework asks whether their prediction architectures can be faithfully aligned&#44; which theoretical differences actually generate their contrasting experimental predictions&#44; and whether these differences carry the particular claims the experiments aim to test&#46;
Building on this analysis&#44; the Channel Standard develops criteria for attributing evidence from competing theory predictions to specific theoretical claims&#46; The broader aim is to move beyond theory&#45;level comparison toward methodological concensus&#46;
