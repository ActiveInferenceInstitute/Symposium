# The Screen as a Markov Blanket&#58; An Active Inference Account of Feed&#45;Driven Narrowing&#44; and a Personal Instrument to Measure It

Presenter: Rajkaushik Borgohain

Contribution: Pre&#45;recorded Video presentation

Public ID: talk&#45;e0738f0a19674084

## Submitted abstract

Short&#45;form video feeds are now a major channel of belief formation&#44; yet cognitive security is often studied at the level of institutions and populations rather than individuals&#46; Albarracin et al&#46; &#40;2022&#41; showed with active inference how echo chambers can emerge among peers&#46; We take a first step toward the individual case&#58; one person and an engagement&#45;optimising recommender&#46;
We treat the phone screen as a Markov blanket&#46; What a person is shown forms the sensory states&#44; and what they do forms the active states&#46; We sketch a minimal user generative model in GNN and couple it with a simple recommender in pymdp&#44; to explore when such a loop keeps a feed diverse and when it narrows or escalates&#46;
We present early simulations and outline a privacy&#45;preserving&#44; on&#45;device instrument through which Worldview aims to make these dynamics visible to people&#58; awareness as a product&#46;
