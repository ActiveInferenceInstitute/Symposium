# Maladaptive Daydreaming as a Precision Trap&#58; An Active Inference Framework&#44; Its Testable Core&#44; and Evidence from Daily Diaries

Presenter: Bo Lee

Contribution: Pre&#45;recorded Video presentation

## Submitted abstract

Maladaptive daydreaming &#40;MD&#41; involves prolonged immersion in self&#45;generated fantasy and difficulty disengaging despite awareness that the experience is imagined&#46; We propose a testable active&#45;inference framework in which the precision assigned to internal predictions relative to external sensory evidence may contribute to self&#45;sustaining fantasy&#46; This precision&#45;feedback mechanism is a hypothesis&#44; not an established clinical explanation&#46; Using analytic counterexamples and 115&#44;200 synthetic datasets&#44; we show why finite&#45;time persistence and hysteresis&#45;like observations alone do not identify bistability&#44; and examine how ramp&#45;and&#45;hold experimental designs can improve discrimination among competing models&#46; Exploratory secondary analyses of daily&#45;diary data are discussed at their actual timescale&#44; without treating daily associations as proof of an episode&#45;level mechanism&#46; The talk connects active inference&#44; computational psychiatry&#44; experimental design&#44; and open science&#46; It concludes with falsifiable predictions and a research agenda focused on measuring internal and sensory precision&#44; tracking within&#45;episode dynamics&#44; comparing alternative models&#44; and testing feasible interventions&#46; We welcome discussion of practical measurements and alternative explanations&#46;
