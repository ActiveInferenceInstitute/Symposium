# What bounds the surprisal&#63; Zones of Bounded Surprisal&#44; self&#45;maintenance and the informational turn in anthropogenesis

Presenter: Tao Gong

Contribution: Pre&#45;recorded Video presentation

Public ID: talk&#45;17082b710a933608

## Submitted abstract

The zone of bounded surprisal &#40;ZBS&#41;&#44; introduced by Manrique and Walker and developed with Friston&#44; describes how much surprising input an agent can absorb under active inference&#46; A narrow zone has been proposed to limit the faithful copying of novel behaviour in great apes&#44; and very precise priors&#44; which narrow the zone&#44; to characterise discriminatory minds&#46; In active&#45;inference terms the zone is the set of outcomes whose surprisal stays under a bound&#46; What sets that bound&#44; and why crossing it costs the agent anything&#44; is left open&#46; We take up this question using four papers published or accepted in 2026 that develop Terrence Deacon&#8217;s teleodynamic account of information&#46; A self&#45;produced Markov blanket institutes the epistemic cut&#44; so the zone belongs to the system that draws its own boundary&#46; Function&#44; understood as the at&#45;risk regeneration of a capacity&#44; gives that system a stake&#46; The bound can then be read as the largest surprisal an agent absorbs without putting this capacity at risk&#44; while precision sets how wide the zone is beneath it&#46; Read together with Walker&#8217;s Homo informatio model&#44; the account explains why width pays&#58; constructive information saves work later and for others&#44; and only an agent with a wide zone can hold it long enough to learn its use&#46; It also raises a testable tension&#46; The constructive information that a broader zone made possible over evolution may narrow the zone within a life when it is held with high precision&#44; and informational inbreeding may be its counterpart at group scale&#46; We propose three measurements&#44; including one that holds surprisal fixed while varying what it threatens&#44; and close with questions for discussion&#46;
