# Version 3.4.0 — Final 2026 Presenter Program

The final *2026 Active Inference Symposium — Presenter Program* was published
on Zenodo on November 21, 2026 at [10.5281/zenodo.24409077](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24409077). It supersedes
[version 3.3.0](release_3_3_0.md) under the
[all-versions book DOI](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23986504). The
[GitHub v3.4.0 release](https://github.com/ActiveInferenceInstitute/Symposium/releases/tag/v3.4.0) attaches the same six files.

## Program and credits

The November 21, 2026 UTC organizer snapshot projects 55 contributions, 53 supplied
abstracts, 19 UTC sessions and 131 consent-filtered public participant profiles.
The prior edition contained 119 public profiles.
The abstracts for Karl Friston and Mike Levin remain pending.

- Jesse Parent and Avery Lim — “From Minimal Model Systems to World Models:
  Boundary-Relative Distinctions for Factoring Generative Models”
- Anna Pereira — “A First Principle Perspective for AI Safety”

The 56 book creators start with Active Inference Institute and include each
named presenter once.
Jesse Parent and Avery Lim receive their supplied ORCIDs and JOPRO affiliation.
The program retains the official presenter credits. The accompanying video cards
also acknowledge Don Trubshaw with Colin Turfus, Hangjun Chu with Tao Gong, and
Sebastian Diaconescu with Diaconescu Adrian-Stelian.
