# Input and projection

Organizers map distinct source tables for participant intake, presentations, and
sessions. The source snapshot and review ledger stay in a private workspace. The
projection derives presenter dossiers from supplied abstracts and attributed source
material. Participant profiles require affirmative public-sharing consent and retain
allowlisted self-reports. Contact and private source identifiers are excluded, and
public identifiers are independent of private row identifiers.

A missing abstract remains visible as pending. Only explicit sessions with valid
UTC times become calendar records. A source update can remove records, change
fields, or alter artifacts. The comparison reports these changes for review, with
identity ambiguity and interpretation left to organizers. Readiness evaluates
structural completeness and preserves the distinction from source freshness.
