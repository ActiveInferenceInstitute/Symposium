# A derived self

Presenter: Viraj Deshwal

Contribution: Pre&#45;recorded Video presentation

## Submitted abstract

A substrate that derives its own body in its own camera through published retinal biology running on hardware&#46; The reafference ladder M0 to M3 is the engineering sequence&#58; discretize action with a move&#45;stop policy&#44; let the body report its own arrival through proprioceptive timing&#44; make arrival predictable&#44; and identify the body as a place through sensitize&#45;adapt dynamics&#46;
The retinal circuit is the one measured in salamander retina by Kastner and Baccus &#40;2013&#41; and others&#44; with thirteen published constants carried at their measured values&#46; Nothing in the chain is tuned&#46; The release time constant arrives on our camera at 2&#46;35 seconds against the paper&#8217;s 2&#46;40&#44; over 98 continuous movement offsets in a novel room with direct sun and moving shadow&#46; The body falls out as the region of the image that stays still while everything touching it moves&#46; No markers&#44; no CAD model&#44; no training set&#46;
The talk will present the implementation&#44; the pre&#45;registered falsification conditions&#44; and the honest limits&#46; τ&#95;on fails against the instrument ceiling&#46; One parameter is not derived&#46; The core observation worth the room&#8217;s attention is that the policy chosen at M0 for credit assignment also solves self&#45;localization at M3&#46; One policy&#46; Two problems usually treated as separate&#46; Active inference predicts this in principle through information&#45;maximizing policy selection&#44; but the specific case of move&#45;stop resolving both at once appears to be new to the literature&#46;
