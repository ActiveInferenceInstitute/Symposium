## Submitted abstract

As AI systems increasingly mediate access to scientific knowledge&#44; how they communicate complex theories becomes consequential for public understanding&#46; Active inference presents a distinctive challenge&#58; its mathematical and computational foundations offer explanatory resources&#44; yet accessible accounts must distinguish formal results&#44; empirical evidence&#44; and emerging interpretations&#46; Simplification can support understanding&#44; but can also obscure uncertainty or encourage misleading conclusions about personal experience&#46;
Building on Pereira and Chamberlin&#8217;s manuscript&#44; this foundational study will explore recommendations for large AI companies communicating active inference to general audiences&#46; Its central question is how AI communication can preserve scientific integrity while helping people understand both the framework&#39;s core concepts and their potential relevance to everyday life&#46; Particular attention will be given to the difference between being able to repeat an explanation and developing an understanding that supports reflection&#44; judgment&#44; and appropriate application&#46;
The project will draw on literature spanning active inference&#44; science communication&#44; human&#8211;AI interaction&#44; and learning&#46; It will examine candidate communication approaches&#44; including layered explanations&#44; everyday examples&#44; explicit statements of uncertainty&#44; and prompts that connect abstract concepts to users&#39; existing knowledge&#46; A potential evaluation framework could consider conceptual accuracy&#44; accessibility&#44; perceived personal relevance&#44; and the ability to distinguish supported implications from speculation&#46; This framework could guide empirical evaluation of approaches&#46;
The proposed contribution is an initial set of communication principles and research questions for AI developers&#44; researchers&#44; and educators&#46; Rather than assuming that greater accessibility necessarily produces better understanding&#44; the study will examine the tensions between clarity&#44; scientific fidelity&#44; personal relevance&#44; and unwarranted certainty&#46; The broader aim is to support communication that enables people to engage meaningfully with complex science without presenting a theoretical framework as a comprehensive explanation of their lives&#46; This thirty minute presentation will outline the rationale&#44; proposed framework&#44; and recommendations emerging from the work&#44; alongside priorities for further research and evaluation&#46;
