# Seeing the System Together&#58; Active Inference for Networks That Learn Together

Presenter: Michael Lennon

Contribution: Pre&#45;recorded Video presentation

Public ID: talk&#45;761def3ebf0aefd9

## Submitted abstract

Imagine the Economics Nobel in 2036 is awarded for the 1st time to a community&#58; the Active Inference Community&#46; Scientists&#44; communities&#44; and institutions now tackle tangled social and ecological challenges more reliably&#44; because they learn together within&#44; between&#44; and beyond their disciplines&#46; What did we build to get there&#63;
This talk works backwards from that future&#44; building on last year&#8217;s invitation to grow the learning capacity of our networks&#46; It proposes machine&#45;assisted collective learning infrastructure enabled by active inference&#58; an organ through which diverse networks sense&#44; make sense&#44; infer&#44; compass&#44; and coordinate adaptive decisions across ways of knowing&#46;
Its architecture is a loop of Evidence&#44; Dialogue&#44; and Adapt&#44; circling a field of memory three layers deep at every scale&#46; The map holds what is measured and recorded&#46; The compass holds interior perceptions and shared hopes that orient action&#44; even in the dark&#46; The ground holds what we inherit&#44; biologically and culturally&#44; from immune memory to language and norms&#46; Each layer is evidence of a different kind&#44; and none certifies another&#46; Friedman&#8217;s meta&#45;pragmatic mechanisms&#44; defined for a single agent and extended here to groups&#44; arbitrate how far to trust each layer and when to revise it&#46;
Stories from development practice&#44; ecology&#44; and network science serve as modelling targets&#58; project dialects that bind teams and wall them off&#44; lessons trapped at the wrong scale&#44; forests managed for what could be measured&#46; Today&#8217;s dialogue&#44; it turns out&#44; is tomorrow&#8217;s inheritance&#46;
The talk is frank about AI&#8217;s double edge&#58; pre&#45;trained models are canned&#44; but the medium around them can learn&#44; and the same tools can homogenise dialogue&#44; breed confident wrongness&#44; or let agents narrate their own history&#46;
Listeners will leave with a flipped version of an old parable&#44; fresh metaphors for explaining active inference&#44; and an invitation to help build the organ&#46;
